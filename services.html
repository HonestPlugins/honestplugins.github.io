How it works

Four steps, no surprises

The process exists to stop the two things that ruin this kind of work: a scope that grows silently, and a bill nobody agreed to.

1 · You write
Tell me what the shop sells, how it sells it, and what is going wrong. A staging URL helps but is not required to start.
2 · I look
I check the real state of the store and tell you what the job actually involves — including if the answer is that you do not need me.
3 · Fixed quote
A written price and scope before any work begins. If it turns out bigger mid-way, we agree the change or we stop. The number does not move on its own.
4 · Delivery
The work, on staging first where one exists, with a short written summary of what changed and why. Then it goes live.

Where I am honest about limits. The VAT work covers Spanish and intra-Community rules, which is what the plugin implements today. I am not a tax adviser and I do not replace your accountant — I make the store apply the rules your accountant tells you apply.
