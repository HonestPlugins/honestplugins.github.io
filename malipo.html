Honest Plugin / Malipo

A payment counts when there is a receipt.

M-Pesa for WooCommerce through Safaricom's Daraja API. Malipo marks an order paid against a real M-Pesa receipt and a matching amount — never on "request accepted" — and when a callback never arrives, it goes and asks until it gets a definitive answer.

In development · not on sale yet

Specification

The failure it is built around

Most lost M-Pesa sales are not failed payments. They are successful payments that the store never recorded, because the callback got lost and nothing went looking for it. That is the problem Malipo exists to close.

Safaricom Daraja API Pay Bill & Till WooCommerce Kenya DPA 2019 In development

Confirmation
An order is marked paid against a real M-Pesa receipt and a matching amount. Never on "request accepted".
Reconciliation
If the callback does not arrive, Malipo queries M-Pesa until it has a definitive answer, instead of leaving the order hanging.
Recovery
When the STK prompt fails, the customer is shown the Pay Bill number and the reference — and the manual payment matches itself to the order.
Validator
Checks credentials, callback URL, permalinks and cron before you go live, so setup mistakes surface on your terms.
Payment page
Standalone — no theme, no framework, no jQuery. Built for a phone on a slow connection where the customer pays for data by the megabyte.
Privacy
Payer phone numbers are masked on every surface, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019.
Credentials
Daraja credentials are stored encrypted in the merchant's own database.

Free vs Pro

The whole payment cycle is free

The complaint on record against the paid M-Pesa plugins is that merchants pay a lot for very little. So the entire billing cycle — STK Push, receipt confirmation, automatic reconciliation, the validators and the C2B fallback — is in the free build and stays there. Pro is about selling in places that are not your shop.
