Specification

What it does, in detail

Built for stores that sell wholesale or B2B — or both B2B and B2C at once — where the wholesale customer must see a net price and the retail customer a price with tax included, without chaining five plugins together.

WordPress 6.6+ PHP 8.0+ WooCommerce English · Español Version 0.1.0

Pricing
Unlimited B2B groups, each with its own discount. Net price per role in shop, product page and cart. Per-product and per-variation overrides.
European VAT
VIES validation that never blocks an order when the service is down. Intra-Community reverse charge. Spanish equivalence surcharge. One-click Spanish VAT setup.
Performance
Prices are precalculated, not computed per request — carts of several hundred lines do not slow the store down.
Registration
B2B sign-up capturing company name, VAT number and country, held for manual admin approval.
Visibility
Hide prices and purchasing from guests, or from everyone who is not an approved B2B customer.
Licensing
When a Pro licence lapses your configured prices keep working. You lose updates and support, not your store.
Uninstall
Deleting the plugin removes its own data, but never the tax records on invoices you already issued, nor the VAT rates it seeded into your store.

Stated limitation. Price hiding covers the storefront, but WooCommerce's public Store API endpoint is not filtered yet — a client calling it directly can still read prices the store meant to hide. This is a documented pending fix, and it is written in the plugin's own FAQ too.
