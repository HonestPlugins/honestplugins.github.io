How these are built

Three rules that do not bend

Scope

Fewer features, all of them working

A long feature list is easy to write and hard to keep correct. Reliability is the feature here, and saying no to the next one is what keeps the existing ones working.

Licensing

A lapsed licence never breaks a store

When a licence expires you stop receiving updates and support. Nothing that was already running switches off. Holding a live store hostage is not a business model.

Disclosure

The gaps are published too

Each plugin's own FAQ states what it does not cover, and what it does not delete when you uninstall it. Nobody should meet a limitation for the first time in production.

Enquiries

Wholesale, payments, or a store that needs cleaning up

Agency terms and volume licences, or setup work — a wholesale catalogue configured with correct intra-Community VAT, M-Pesa taking payments on a live store, a compromised site cleaned and hardened. You get a real answer, not a sales sequence.
